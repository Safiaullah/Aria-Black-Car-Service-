Pricing Questions to Ask Before You Book

Never compare providers on the headline fare alone. Ask the same questions every time so you can compare real totals.

  • How much complimentary wait time is included?
  • What is the cancellation window?
  • Is the rate locked, or can it change with traffic, weather, or demand?
