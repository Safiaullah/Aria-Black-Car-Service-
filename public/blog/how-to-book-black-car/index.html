How to Book a Black Car Service in NYC (Step by Step, 2026)

Travel Tips · Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

Booking a black car takes about two minutes if you have the right details ready. Here's the simple process, plus the small things that make the difference between a smooth pickup and a stressful one.

What You'll Need

  • Pickup address (and drop-off, or airport and flight number)
  • Date and time
  • Number of passengers and bags — this sets the vehicle class
  • Any extras: child seats, additional stops, meet-and-greet
  • Contact number for your chauffeur's arrival text

The Four Steps

  1. Request a quote — book online or call (888) 313-2455; you'll get a flat rate
  2. Choose your vehicle — sedan, SUV, or Sprinter based on your group and luggage
  3. Confirm — the rate is locked and a chauffeur is assigned
  4. Ride — your chauffeur arrives early; for airports, they track your flight and meet you inside

How Far Ahead to Book

  • Airport transfers: 24 hours is ideal; online booking requires 12 hours’ notice
  • Weddings and events: book weeks ahead to lock the fleet
  • Peak dates (holidays, marquee events): a week or more
  • Last minute: pickups within 12 hours are arranged by phone — call (888) 313-2455, 24/7, subject to availability

Pro Tips

Add your flight number so we can track delays automatically. For airport pickups, choose meet-and-greet if you want your chauffeur waiting at baggage claim rather than curbside. And save your preferences with an account or corporate profile so future bookings take seconds.
