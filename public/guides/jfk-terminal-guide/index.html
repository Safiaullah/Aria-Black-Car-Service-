JFK terminals in 2026 — what is actually open

John F. Kennedy International Airport is in the middle of the Port Authority’s $19 billion rebuild. For ground transport, the only map that matters is the one your airline is using today. The official JFK terminals directory lists five operating passenger terminals: 1, 4, 5, 7, and 8. Terminal 2 is not on that list.

The Port Authority’s JFK redevelopment page states that new Terminal 1 and new Terminal 6 have first gates scheduled to open in 2026, and that completed Terminal 6 will occupy the site of the former Terminal 6 and today’s Terminal 7. Until your airline actually moves you, treat T7 as open and T2 as closed. Recheck the airline app within 24 hours of departure.

Per-terminal pickup notes for Aria

Aria chauffeurs meet you inside arrivals with a name sign — not in a rideshare lot and not with a solicitor in the baggage hall. Add the airline and flight number when you book so we track the landing and hold the complimentary 60-minute airport wait. If immigration or a delayed bag runs long, stay in the public arrivals hall and call (888) 402-8202.

Terminal 1 — international arrivals, New Terminal One construction

T1 is an international terminal (Lufthansa, Air France, Korean Air, SWISS, Turkish, and other carriers on the official directory). After you land: complete U.S. Customs and Border Protection, collect bags, then walk into public arrivals. The chauffeur meets you there. Roadways around T1 are part of the New Terminal One work zone — leave extra time for drop-offs, and do not assume a rideshare pin from a previous trip is still legal.

Terminal 2 — closed

Terminal 2 is closed and demolished. It does not appear on the official JFK terminals or airlines pages. If a hotel confirmation, old blog, or forwarded itinerary says “T2,” ignore it and open your airline’s current terminal. There is no Aria pickup at T2 because there is no T2.

Terminal 4 — Delta, Emirates, and international meet-and-greet past customs

T4 is JFK’s largest operating terminal and Delta’s hub. It is also home to Emirates, Virgin Atlantic, Etihad, KLM, Singapore Airlines, and many other long-haul carriers. Walks between far A and B gates are long; JFKIAT publishes walk times of up to 10–14 minutes from security to the outer gates.

International meet-and-greet: you must finish CBP and collect checked bags before the chauffeur can meet you. The meeting point is the public arrivals hall past customs, not at the jet bridge and not airside. Look for the Aria sign with your name. Domestic Delta arrivals meet after baggage claim in the same public arrivals area. If your party splits between a Sky Club and baggage, send one person to the agreed hall and tell dispatch.

Terminal 5 — JetBlue, with a construction caveat

T5 is JetBlue’s flagship terminal (also Cape Air and Sun Country). Most JetBlue arrivals — including international arrivals from 5:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. — use T5. The official airline directory sends JetBlue international arrivals from 10:30 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. to Terminal 4; put the flight number on the reservation so we are not waiting at the wrong hall.

The official T5 page states that ride-app pickup has been temporarily relocated to the Howard Beach AirTrain station because of construction. That is a rideshare-zone change. Aria is not an app-lot pickup: the chauffeur still meets you inside T5 arrivals, then walks you to the car at the legal commercial curb.

Terminal 7 — open, but in transition

T7 is still an operating terminal on the official JFK site. Airlines listed there include Aer Lingus, Air Canada, ANA, Condor, Ethiopian, Frontier, Icelandair, Kuwait, and Norse Atlantic. The official T7 page also says ride-app pickup has been relocated to Howard Beach AirTrain during construction.

Port Authority materials describe new Terminal 6 as eventually occupying the former T6 and T7 sites, with first new-terminal gates scheduled for 2026. We do not treat T7 as closed, and we do not guess a move date for your flight. If you are booked on a T7 airline, we plan for T7 unless the airline app says otherwise that day.

Terminal 8 — American, British Airways, and oneworld partners

T8 is American Airlines’ JFK hub. The official airline directory also lists British Airways, Alaska, Qatar, Iberia, Finnair, Japan Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Qantas, and other T8 carriers. Meet after baggage claim in public arrivals. T8 has been expanded and is getting a commercial refresh; follow current arrivals signage rather than a photo from an older trip.

AirTrain JFK: free inside the airport, fare at Jamaica and Howard Beach

The official JFK AirTrain page is the source for fares and rules:

  • Free between passenger terminals and for on-airport trips (parking, Federal Circle hotel shuttles / rental cars, Lefferts Boulevard).
  • $8.75 one-way when you enter or exit at Jamaica Station or Howard Beach Station — the two exits that connect to the subway and, at Jamaica, Long Island Rail Road.
  • Children under five ride free. Contactless / OMNY payment is accepted at those fare gates.
  • JFK also publishes a rolling 30-day AirTrain fare cap of $43.50 on the same payment method, and a group fare (first rider $8.75, additional riders $4.50, maximum $26.75).

Pair AirTrain with the subway and the official MTA base fare (effective January 4, 2026) is $3.00, so a typical AirTrain + subway journey is about $11.75 per person before any LIRR ticket. Jamaica connects to the E, J, and Z; Howard Beach connects to the A. The ride is the cheapest door-adjacent option only if you can handle stairs, transfers, and your own luggage. It is not meet-and-greet.

AirTrain + subway — lowest cash outlay, longest handling of bags, and no one waiting if a connection is missed. Fine for a light backpack. A poor fit after an international flight with two large suitcases.
