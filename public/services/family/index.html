NYC Family Car Service

Family-friendly black car service across NYC with complimentary child seats, extra luggage room, and patient, vetted chauffeurs.

Traveling with kids in New York is its own logistics puzzle — car seats, strollers, extra bags, and the anxiety of a curbside rideshare that shows up with no way to secure a toddler. Aria's family car service solves all of it in one booking: request the child seats you need when you reserve, and your chauffeur arrives with them already installed and inspected.

Child Seats, Installed and Inspected

We provide the correct seat for every age and stage, at no extra charge, installed before pickup so you're never wrestling with LATCH anchors on a sidewalk with a flight to catch:

  • Rear-facing infant seats — for newborns and infants up to ~35 lbs
  • Convertible toddler seats — forward or rear-facing for 1–4 year-olds
  • High-back & backless boosters — for school-age children up to ~4'9"

Just tell us your children's ages at booking and we handle the rest. Traveling with two or three? A Premium SUV or Executive Sprinter gives you the room for multiple seats plus a double stroller.

Airport Travel With Kids, Made Calm

Family airport trips are where Aria earns its keep. Every transfer includes real-time flight tracking and 60 minutes of complimentary wait time, so a slow bag carousel or a diaper change in arrivals never costs you the ride. Your chauffeur meets you inside at baggage claim with a name sign, helps load luggage and gear, and gets everyone buckled before you pull away. Flat rates from Manhattan run $165 to JFK, $140 to LaGuardia, and $180 to Newark — the same price at 5 a.m. with a red-eye as at rush hour.

Chauffeurs Parents Trust

Every Aria chauffeur is NYC TLC-licensed, background-checked, and trained to drive smoothly and patiently — no sudden stops, no rushing a family that's still getting settled. Vehicles are non-smoking, sanitized between trips, and stocked with bottled water and phone chargers. Over 5,000 completed trips at a 5.0★ rating means we've done the school runs, the hospital discharges with a newborn, and the multi-generational airport pickups.
