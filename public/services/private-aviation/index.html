NYC Private Aviation Ground Transportation

Seamless ground transportation for private aviation — planeside and FBO transfers at Teterboro, Westchester, JFK, and the tri-state area.

Private aviation runs on precision, and the ground leg should match the air leg. Aria coordinates directly with FBOs and flight departments to deliver planeside and terminal transfers that are already waiting when your aircraft blocks in — at Teterboro, Westchester, Republic, and every major tri-state airport.

FBO & Planeside Coordination

We work the way flight departments and charter operators do — around the tail number and the ATA, not a rideshare pin:

  • Tail-number tracking with automatic adjustment for early or delayed arrivals
  • Planeside pickup where the FBO permits ramp access
  • Direct coordination with Signature, Meridian, jetAviation, and other FBOs
  • Chauffeur staged and waiting before block-in — zero passenger wait
  • Discreet handling for principals, crews, and VIP passengers

Teterboro, Westchester & Beyond

Teterboro (TEB) is the busiest private-aviation gateway to Manhattan, and it's our home turf — flat sedan transfers to Midtown run $180, SUVs $275, typically 30–45 minutes depending on traffic. We also cover Westchester County (HPN) at $200 sedan / $295 SUV, Republic (FRG) on Long Island, and the private terminals at Newark and JFK. For multi-leg trips, one chauffeur can stay with your principal all day.

The Right Vehicle for the Mission

Choose an Executive or First-Class Sedan (S-Class) for principals traveling solo, a Premium SUV for luggage-heavy or small-group trips, or an Executive Sprinter for full flight crews and larger parties. Every vehicle is immaculate, stocked, and staffed by a TLC-licensed chauffeur who understands the confidentiality private travel demands. NDA-ready chauffeurs available on request.
